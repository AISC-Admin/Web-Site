A Malian company serving the African space industry

A public limited company registered in Bamako, AISC Space Exploration Technologies is building an integrated space sector: launch infrastructure, engineering, energy and research.

Who we are

Our mission

AISC Space Exploration Technologies SA is a company registered in Mali, committed to the development of the African space industry. Its ambition is to strengthen the upstream segment of the space value chain through the progressive deployment of a spaceport in Mali, designed to foster the emergence of African launch and space-access capabilities.

AISC also offers consulting services in the midstream domain, along with engineering, mission design and space systems services. Through its activities, the company aims to contribute to opening Africa to the new space economy and to building a sustainable technological, industrial and scientific ecosystem.

Our areas of activity

A broad, integrated scope of activities

In line with its corporate purpose, AISC covers the entire aerospace value chain, from design to operations.

Aerospace

  • Construction of aircraft for the transport of goods or passengers, for civil, sporting or institutional use
  • Construction of helicopters, gliders and hang gliders
  • Construction of airships and hot-air balloons
  • Manufacture of major assemblies: fuselages, wings, doors, control surfaces, landing gear, tanks, nacelles
  • Manufacture of propellers, rotors and rotor blades for helicopters
  • Manufacture of aircraft engines (turbojets, turboprops) and their parts
  • Manufacture of flight simulators for ground training
  • Maintenance, overhaul, conversion and factory reconstruction of aircraft and engines

Space

  • Construction of spacecraft, their launchers and satellites
  • Construction of planetary probes, orbital stations and space shuttles
  • Launch of spacecraft, satellites and space vehicles
  • Space transport of cargo and people
  • Manufacture of satellite telecommunications equipment
  • Maintenance and repair of spacecraft
  • Design of space missions and systems, strategic consulting (midstream)

Industry & structures

  • Design of metal, boiler-making and piping structures
  • Manufacture of instruments for aviation and aeronautics
  • Air navigation assistance systems and aircraft lighting equipment
  • Equipment and devices for launching aerial vehicles (catapults and similar equipment)

Energy & digital

Energy & research

A solar ambition serving space activities

AISC is working on the deployment of solar power plants to sustainably power its future space infrastructure. This program includes the study of a photovoltaic panel manufacturing unit as well as solar farms intended for both industrial sites and local communities in the regions concerned.

In parallel, AISC aims to offer a research environment close to real conditions for the study of botany and agriculture in a space context — an essential building block for the autonomy of future missions and the continent's scientific ecosystem.

  • Solar power plant program to supply space sites
  • Solar farms benefiting local communities
  • Applied research in space botany and agriculture
Experimental greenhouse & solar plant
Applied research

A laboratory dedicated to space botany

Our research teams study plant growth and resilience under controlled conditions, drawing on soil-free growing and lighting technologies used to prepare future space missions.

This progressive approach — from the laboratory to the terrestrial analogue, then towards conditions ever closer to space — aims to build recognized African expertise in space agriculture, in service of the food security of future missions.
